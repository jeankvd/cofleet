# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in CoFleet a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

We will act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Give and accept constructive technical feedback.
- Respect privacy and avoid posting another person's data without permission.
- Focus on what is best for users and the community.
- Acknowledge mistakes, repair harm where possible, and learn from feedback.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, sexualized language, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, insulting comments, personal attacks, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information, credentials, host details, or conversations.
- Impersonation, deceptive attribution, or misuse of project access.
- Conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone publicly represents the
project. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other material that violates it and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Report sensitive incidents privately through the repository's GitHub private
reporting channel. Reports will be handled with discretion, conflicts of
interest will be avoided, and retaliation against a reporter is prohibited.
